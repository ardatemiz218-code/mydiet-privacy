# MyDiet – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

## 1. Introduction

MyDiet is a personal fitness and nutrition planning application currently under development. It is designed to provide personalized meal plans, nutrition tracking, and fitness recommendations.

## 2. Information We May Collect

With your permission, MyDiet may collect:

- WHOOP recovery, HRV, and resting heart rate data.
- Sleep duration and sleep performance.
- Daily strain and estimated energy expenditure.
- Workout and physical activity data.
- Height, weight, and body measurements.
- Nutrition records and dietary preferences.
- Meal photographs and ingredient information.
- Personal fitness and nutrition goals.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

Information is intended to be used to:

- Generate personalized daily meal plans.
- Calculate estimated calories and nutritional values.
- Adapt nutrition plans to workout schedules.
- Support recovery and healthy sleep habits.
- Track fitness and nutrition progress.

## 4. WHOOP Integration

MyDiet uses the WHOOP API with user authorization. Users may revoke access through their WHOOP account settings.

MyDiet is an independent application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by WHOOP.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

MyDiet plans to use Supabase for secure cloud storage and backend services. Access controls and authentication will be implemented to protect personal information.

MyDiet does not intend to sell personal or health information or use it for advertising.

## 6. Food Photography

Meal photographs may be processed to identify food and estimate portion sizes and nutritional values. These estimates may not be completely accurate.

Any future third-party image analysis or artificial intelligence services will be disclosed before implementation.

## 7. User Rights

Users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information and disconnect their WHOOP account.

## 8. Medical Disclaimer

MyDiet is intended for general fitness and nutritional guidance. It does not provide medical diagnoses or replace professional medical advice.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy will be updated as the application is developed and its data processing practices are finalized.
